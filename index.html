A concept for the future UHNW workspace · for discussion

Meridian

The client is a thread. The day is a plan for your attention.

Today an advisor's work is spread across a CRM, a reporting platform, email, a calendar, slide decks, an order system and a compliance log, and the advisor is the integration layer between them. This concept organises the work by relationship and by time instead, with agents in the conversation as participants who act under visible authority. There are no dashboards to navigate.

What you'll see is one Monday morning in October 2028 and one client event — Eleanor Whitfield writes at 7:42 that the family needs $6.5M for a house closing — followed through the four people and agents who touch it. Eight scenes, about five minutes, narrated.

That isn't it — check the note you were sent.

Use → and ← to move between scenes, Space to play, and click anything the narration mentions. Best on a laptop with sound on. All names, firms and figures are fictional.
